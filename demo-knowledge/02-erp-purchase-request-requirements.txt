DEVELOPERTWIN DEMO KNOWLEDGE — ERP PURCHASE REQUEST FEATURE
Document ID: DEMO-REQ-022 | Sprint target: 2026-Q4 | Status: Draft for analysis
System: ProcureStudio (fictional ERP training application)

BUSINESS GOAL
Allow employees to create purchase requests, route them for the right approvals, and see a complete audit trail. Finance wants to prevent overspending and duplicates.

PERSONAS
- Employee: creates and edits draft requests.
- Team Lead: approves requests up to $10,000.
- Department Head: approves requests above $10,000 through $50,000.
- CFO: approves requests above $50,000.
- Procurement Officer: validates supplier details and quote attachments.

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
REQ-01: Every request has a unique PR number, requester ID, department ID, currency, list of items, supplier candidate, and creation date.
REQ-02: A request starts in Draft. Submit changes status to PendingApproval and freezes the amount/items until rejection or an explicit change request.
REQ-03: Approval route must follow policy DEMO-POL-001, including total amount inclusive of tax.
REQ-04: When total exceeds $25,000, the UI must require at least three vendor-quote attachments unless a documented exception is approved by Procurement Manager.
REQ-05: An approver may Approve or Reject and must supply a comment for rejection.
REQ-06: Every transition writes an immutable audit record with actor, action, previous status, new status and UTC time.
REQ-07: When approved, Procurement may convert the PR into a PO; double submission must not create two POs.
REQ-08: Employees can view their own requests; approvers see requests assigned to them.

VALIDATION AND NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum 1 line item; amounts must be positive; maximum 100 line items.
- Dates and audit timestamps must use UTC.
- API must validate role/authorization, not trust frontend role labels.
- Use optimistic concurrency when two approvers act on the same request.
- Prefer idempotency keys for Submit and Convert-to-PO operations.
- SLA target for retrieving a request by ID: p95 < 500ms in a non-loaded test environment.

OUT-OF-SCOPE
Payment gateway integration, supplier onboarding, accounting ledger posting.

OPEN QUESTIONS
Who can override a rejected request? How are multi-currency exchange rates chosen? Should approvals be serial or parallel for multi-department purchases? These questions need stakeholder decisions before implementation.
